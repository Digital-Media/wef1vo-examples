A Weekend in the Mountains

Two days, one hut and a lot of fresh air: a weekend in the mountains is the perfect break from lectures and screens. You don't need much for it, but you should plan a few things in advance.

What to Pack

  • Hiking boots that you have already worn in
  • A rain jacket, even if the sun is shining in the valley
  • Enough water and something to eat

The weather in the mountains changes quickly. Before you leave, check the weather forecast and tell someone where you are going.
